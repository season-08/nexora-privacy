Introduction

Nexora ("we", "our", or "us") is a study assistant app developed by Nexora Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app. We believe in keeping things simple and transparent.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account. The only data processed is the text, notes, or documents you voluntarily submit within the app for study purposes. We do not collect your name, email, location, or any other personal identifiers.

How We Use Your Data

Content you submit — typed notes, pasted text, or uploaded PDFs — is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and mind maps. This content is processed solely to provide you with study output and is not stored by us after your session.

Third-Party Services

Nexora uses Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic. Your submitted content is processed according to Anthropic's privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/privacy. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

Data Storage

Nexora does not store, sell, or share your personal data. No user accounts or profiles are created. Your content is processed in the moment and not retained on our servers.

Children's Privacy

Nexora is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our app, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
